A coast-to-coast trip through the Pyrenees, crossing constantly from Spain to France, and back. Seeing everything that we have to see.

## The Journey

From the Atlantic to the Mediterranean, weaving through mountain passes that define the border between Spain and France. Every turn brings a new view, a new country, and a new adventure.

## Highlights

- **Cross-border riding** — Constantly switching between Spain and France
- **Mountain passes** — Some of the best riding in Europe
- **Coast to coast** — Atlantic Ocean to Mediterranean Sea
- **Local culture** — Basque Country, Catalonia, and everything in between
