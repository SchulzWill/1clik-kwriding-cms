Our trip to the fascinating Picos de Europa, in Spain. Going to the mountains, Covadonga lakes and church, and a lot of curves.

{{< youtube dQw4w9WgXcQ >}}

## Highlights

- **Covadonga Lakes** — Glacial lakes surrounded by dramatic peaks
- **Covadonga Church** — Historic sanctuary in the mountains
- **Endless curves** — Some of the best motorcycle roads in Spain
- **Picos de Europa National Park** — Wild, rugged mountain landscapes

## The Riding

The Picos de Europa offer some of the most rewarding riding in Northern Spain. Tight curves, dramatic elevation changes, and views that will make you stop at every mirador. This is what motorcycle touring is all about.
