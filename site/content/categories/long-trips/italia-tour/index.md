Passing through the Italian and Swiss Alps, including Splugenpass, Stelvio, Passo Sella, Passo Guarda, Dolomites, Genova, Milano, Como, Bellagio, Menaggio, Lugano, Splugen, Bormio, Bolzano, Verona, Bologna, Firenze, Siena, Toscana and Roma.

## Highlights

- **Stelvio Pass** — One of the most famous mountain passes in the world
- **Dolomites** — Breathtaking UNESCO World Heritage landscapes
- **Passo Sella & Passo Guarda** — Epic alpine riding
- **Lake Como** — Bellagio, Menaggio, and stunning lakeside roads
- **Tuscany** — Rolling hills, Siena, and Firenze
- **Roma** — The Eternal City

## Route

Genova → Milano → Como → Bellagio → Menaggio → Lugano → Splugen → Splügenpass → Bormio → Stelvio → Bolzano → Dolomites → Passo Sella → Passo Guarda → Verona → Bologna → Firenze → Siena → Toscana → Roma
