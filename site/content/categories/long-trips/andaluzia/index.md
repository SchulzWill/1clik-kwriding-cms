Amazing trip through Andalucia, going to Sevilla, Granada, Sierra Nevada and the Tabernas Desert.

## Highlights

- **Sevilla** — Flamenco, tapas, and stunning architecture
- **Granada** — The magnificent Alhambra and historic Albaicín
- **Sierra Nevada** — Spain's highest mountain range and incredible roads
- **Tabernas Desert** — Europe's only true desert, famous for Western movies

## The Experience

Southern Spain delivers incredible diversity — from the vibrant cities of Sevilla and Granada to the snow-capped peaks of Sierra Nevada, all the way to the surreal landscapes of the Tabernas Desert. Every kilometer tells a different story.
